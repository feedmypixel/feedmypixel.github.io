# FeedMyPixel — Home (brochure) design brief

**For:** [claude.ai/design](https://claude.ai/design). Builds on `brief-global.md` (do that
first — reuse its tokens, type scale, header, footer). Deliver the **home page** as a
self-contained HTML + CSS prototype, light theme primary (dark a bonus), desktop + mobile.

## Goal of this page

A hiring lead or fellow developer lands and, within seconds, gets: **who Ben is, the calibre
of his work, that he ships his own products, and how to hire him** — plus a reason to subscribe.
The page itself must feel *simple, well-made, quality*.

## Sections (top → bottom)

1. **Hero.** Who Ben is + the value, fast. Senior contract full-stack engineer; *simple,
   well-made, quality products.* One strong primary CTA (Hire / Get in touch). Calm, confident,
   lots of space. Lean on the pixel motif subtly.

2. **What I do (services).** A scannable set: full-stack build, web design, architecture,
   dev-ops / infrastructure, accessibility, design systems. Short, concrete — not buzzwords.

3. **Selected work / proof.** Credibility through pedigree: Guardian, BBC, HMRC/GDS, DEFRA,
   Wayfair, and the current WhiskyInvestDirect SvelteKit rebuild. Summary-level (no deep case
   studies in v1) — logos/names + a line each. Prominent link through to the full **Work /
   experience explorer** (see `brief-experience.md`). Design it to scale to more later.

4. **Products.** Proof he ships his own: **Pipes** (live — Chrome Web Store + GitHub links) and
   **stat** (coming soon). Small product cards.

5. **Latest writing.** 2–3 most-recent blog posts (title, date, summary) → link to the blog.

6. **Contact / hire + newsletter.** Clear primary contact (email — always actionable, never a
   dead-end). Plus a short "about Ben" teaser linking to /about, and the newsletter signup
   (email capture). Show success + error states for the signup inline.

## Notes

- Mobile-first; show both. Keep it fast and light (works as plain HTML/CSS).
- Don't dead-end the user — every section points somewhere useful.
- One clear primary action per view (the hire CTA); secondary actions stay quieter.

## Deliver

The full home page, all sections, light theme (dark optional), desktop + mobile, annotated where
it deviates from or extends the global tokens.
