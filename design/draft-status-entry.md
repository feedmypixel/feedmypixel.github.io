## Story

### Problem

Keeping in touch takes more than it should. A full post feels like a performance and a message out
of the blue needs a reason.

### Solution

status keeps it small. Post an emoji and a few words and your people can react, reply or message you
for a natter. A small update is all it takes to start a conversation.

### Product

A web app at status.me, with iPhone and Android apps on the way. SvelteKit and Svelte 5 in front of
a Fastify and PostgreSQL api on Fly.io. Sign in by email link or passkey, no passwords. Push
notifications on every platform and fingerprint or face app lock. No ads and no tracking for
advertising. For 13 and over.
