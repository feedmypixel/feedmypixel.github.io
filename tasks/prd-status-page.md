# PRD: status product page and Products carousel rework

Source: `design_handoff_status_v7` (Claude Design, 13 Nov 2026), pinned at `design/status-v7/mocks-pinned/`.
The render is the spec. Where this file and the render disagree, the render wins unless a decision
below says otherwise. Build inventory: `design/status-v7/inventory.md`.

**Not for release.** Built on `feat/product-pages`, which also carries the Pipes page; both release together. Nothing merges to `main` (which deploys) until the
status apps are through store review and Ben says go.

## 1. Overview

status is Ben's second product under feedMyPixel, after Pipes. Maker communities will land on a page
that explains what status is and tells the build story: how and why Ben built it, with Claude, on
SvelteKit. The home page Products section gains a status entry and both product carousels move from
pre-framed images to slides composed live, with screenshots that follow the site theme.

## 2. Goals

1. Ship `/status`: hero, screenshots, problem / solution / product, build story.
2. Add status to home Products, above Pipes, linking to `/status`.
3. Rework both home carousels to composed slides whose screenshots follow light and dark.
4. Spec diff against the pinned mocks: 0 defects (or reasoned waivers) at 375 and 1440, both themes.

## 3. User stories

- As a maker who followed a link, I can read what status is, see it and read how it was built.
- As a visitor on the home page, I see both products and can reach the status story.
- As a visitor in dark mode, every screenshot I see is in dark mode, without a flash of light ones.
- As a keyboard or screen-reader user, the carousels, strips and table of contents are operable
  and announced, as on the rest of the site.

## 4. Functional requirements

### /status

1. Prerendered route at `/status` inside the existing header, footer and theme.
2. Hero: breadcrumb, pixel pair beside h1 "status", pitch, lead, actions, fact line (render copy).
3. Screenshots band: the home status carousel (decision 24), then Problem / Solution / Product.
4. Build story: sticky table of contents and stack list from 960px; article with five sections,
   the web app carousel inside "How it's built" (decision 23), the tip box and the repeated actions.
5. More from feedMyPixel: removed (decision 19).
6. Share image `https://status.me/og.png`, title "status - an emoji and your status | feedMyPixel".
7. Listed in `sitemap.xml`.

### Home Products

8. status entry first, Pipes second with a top rule, same anatomy (pixel pair, h3, lead, actions,
   carousel, story).
9. Composed slides for both carousels (caption column plus screenshot column), stacking below
   760px, per the README tables.
10. status Product copy without Fly.io (render copy).
11. `static/pipes/framed-*.png` removed.

### Shared

12. Screenshots follow the site theme with no flash and no JavaScript: both images are in the
    markup, CSS shows the one matching `html[data-theme]` and the hidden one is lazy so it is not
    fetched. status slide 02 uses the opposite theme.
13. Store links are the site's own buttons (decision 1). status shows App Store and Google Play
    buttons only once their URLs exist in `config.ts`.

## 6. Non-goals

- Release, merging to `main` or adding status to `llms.txt` before go.
- A blog. Native app pages. A status-branded look (the page uses feedMyPixel's system).
- Changing the header, footer, hero, Experience, Services or Contact.

## 7. Design considerations

Tokens only, scoped component CSS, existing bricks first (`Button`, `Carousel`, `Seo`,
`BackToTop`). New bricks: `PixelMark`, `ProductStory`, `ThemedImage`, the four `/status` sections.

## 8. Technical considerations

- `Carousel` gains a slide snippet and an offset-based strip mode; pure maths lives in
  `carousel.ts` with tests.
- Design check: `pnpm design:check` with `design/element-map.json`.
- Hero section carries class `hero` so the scroll reveal in `patterns.css` skips it.

## 9. Success metrics

- Spec diff empty or waived with reasons, 2 surfaces x 2 viewports x 2 themes.
- Paired gallery viewed. Unit, e2e (incl. axe) and visual tests green. Lighthouse not worse than home.
